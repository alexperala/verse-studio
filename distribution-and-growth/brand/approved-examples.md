# Verse Studio Approved Voice Examples

Last updated: 2026-07-15

These examples have been explicitly selected by Alex as strong expressions of the intended public voice. Consult them before drafting public copy. Do not reuse them mechanically; infer the emotional logic beneath them.

## Approved Anchors

> the village is quiet tonight. nothing has been formally alleged

Why it works:

- begins with passive, peaceful observation;
- implies private uncertainty without explaining it;
- uses formal language as emotional cover rather than broad satire;
- remains restrained and leaves the imagined situation unresolved.

> a little film for people who would like to leave without creating a logistical situation

Why it works:

- expresses yearning through an awkward practical concern;
- is emotionally recognizable without becoming confessional;
- makes the invitation gentle and non-imposing;
- promotes the work while sounding like a person rather than a campaign.

## Explicit Negative Examples

These were identified by Alex as directions to avoid:

> the mountain has declined to comment

> winter has entered the project without leaving comments

They make external objects perform an institutional joke. The satire arrives before interior feeling, which pushes the voice away from yearning, awkwardness, and passive observation.

## Approved Social Caption Structure

Keep promotional captions in one voice:

> voiced line
>
> full video: URL

The destination line should be separated by whitespace and reduced to the minimum useful information. Do not follow the voiced line with a longer explanatory sentence that sounds like conventional campaign copy.

Add future examples only after Alex approves or clearly favors them.
