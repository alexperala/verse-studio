# Measurement

## What Success Means

The long-term aim is a growing pool of people who recognize Verse Studio, choose to spend time with the work, and return. Treat raw reach as an input, not the outcome.

Measure the funnel in layers:

| Layer | Primary questions | Useful signals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Distribution | Was the work shown to a relevant audience? | Impressions, reach, traffic source, placement |
| Packaging | Did the intended audience choose it? | Click-through rate, view starts, profile visits |
| Experience | Did the work hold attention? | Watch time, average view duration, average percentage viewed, completion, rewatches |
| Connection | Did viewers want more? | Subscribers or follows, returning viewers, meaningful comments, saves, shares |
| Compounding | Did the release help the catalogue? | Views of other works, later organic returns, search and Suggested growth |

For long ambient films, emphasize qualified watch time and absolute average view duration alongside percentage viewed.

## Snapshot Schedule

Capture data near:

- pre-launch baseline when the channel already exists;
- 24 hours;
- 72 hours;
- 7 days;
- 14 days;
- 30 days;
- later only when a material change or experiment warrants it.

Use the closest available time and record the actual timestamp. Never rewrite a prior row.

## Diagnosis

- **Low impressions, strong viewing:** the audience signal or catalogue may be too small; continue coherent distribution before repackaging reflexively.
- **Impressions, weak clicks:** test a more accurate or compelling title-thumbnail combination.
- **Clicks, early abandonment:** packaging may set the wrong expectation, or the opening may not deliver it.
- **Good viewing, weak connection:** make the channel promise and next action clearer; provide a related next work when available.
- **Paid views, no later organic behavior:** the campaign purchased exposure but did not find a durable audience.

Do not declare a format successful or failed from one post or a tiny sample. Record confidence and alternative explanations.

## Attribution Rules

- Separate organic and paid traffic wherever the platform permits.
- Record spend, targeting, creative, and campaign dates for every paid observation.
- Treat correlated organic growth during paid activity as a hypothesis, not proof of causation.
- Compare like with like: long-form with long-form, Shorts with Shorts, and each platform primarily against its own prior baseline.

## Repository Records

- `distribution-and-growth/analytics/channel-snapshots.csv` stores channel-level observations.
- `distribution-and-growth/analytics/content-performance.csv` stores release- and asset-level observations.
- `distribution-and-growth/analytics/experiments.csv` stores hypotheses, success criteria, results, and decisions.
- The active campaign folder stores qualitative findings and the next decision.
