Confidence is moderate that distribution and conversion—not title or thumbnail dissatisfaction—are the main bottlenecks. Confidence is low about audience subtypes because the samples are small and later retention, returning-viewer, and click data were unavailable.

## What Worked

- The film continued gaining public YouTube views after the initial launch window, rising from 21 on July 25 to 39 by the final July 29 scan.
- Early long-form viewing duration was strong enough to justify preserving the current positioning and packaging.
- Instagram repeatedly reached roughly 100 non-followers per excerpt and produced saves on two of the three Reels.
- The dedicated Verse Ambient channel, Home tab, **Verse Ambient Films** playlist, related-video paths, channel copy, and disclosure workflow were established.
- The campaign produced reusable horizontal and vertical derivatives without compromising the films with overlays or unrelated trends.
- A distinctive Verse public voice and a stricter engagement standard are now documented and should remain invariant across posts and replies.

## What Did Not Work Yet

- Discovery did not convert into profile activity, followers, subscribers, substantive comments, or reliable link clicks.
- The campaign did not establish returning-viewer behavior.
- TikTok delivery was inconsistent, including one verified public post with a persistent zero-view Studio result.
- X produced limited residual reach and no measured link clicks on the posts with detailed analytics.
- YouTube Shorts did not receive meaningful Shorts-feed distribution in the available reads.
- Broader outbound engagement produced little evidence of audience movement. The first round included four follows, five likes, and one reply; a stricter second review found no additional accounts worth publicly curating.

## Current Access Blockers

- Instagram recognized `@verse.videos` but required authentication.
- TikTok was signed out and intermittently failed to load.

Do not request or store credentials. When work resumes, ask Alex to restore the relevant sessions directly in Chrome and then verify the exact account before reading analytics or acting publicly.

## Decisions Carried Forward

1. Keep the existing YouTube title and thumbnail unchanged unless new evidence shows impressions without clicks or a clear expectation mismatch.
2. Use Instagram as the primary surface for the next distinct organic excerpt.
3. Do not mirror that excerpt to TikTok in the same wave; preserve a clean platform comparison.
4. Judge the next Instagram test on at least one profile action or an additional save, not raw views alone.
5. Defer paid promotion until another organic conversion test is complete.
6. Curator or creator outreach remains unapproved and unsent.
7. Preserve the established Verse voice for all posts, replies, comments, and outreach. Read the brand voice files before drafting public language.
