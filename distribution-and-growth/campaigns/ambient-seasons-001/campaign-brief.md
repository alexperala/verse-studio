## Confirmed Creative Facts

- The master film is complete and runs 26 minutes 20 seconds.
- It moves through Japanese countryside scenes across changing seasons.
- The visual style is Japanese anime-inspired.
- The soundtrack was generated with ElevenLabs and shaped as ambient or quiet experimental music.
- The music is chill but not consistently sleep-oriented or purely unobtrusive.
- Alex is happy with the finished film and intends to share it very soon, ideally today.
- Future ambient films may use other styles, including stop motion, fantasy, or different imagined worlds.

## Campaign Goal

Primary goal: begin building a qualified, returning audience for Verse Studio's ambient cinema.

Secondary goals:

- establish a coherent home for future ambient releases;
- learn which audience framing produces meaningful viewing, not merely clicks;
- create a reusable launch-and-measurement practice for future work.

Non-goals:

- immediate monetization;
- maximizing the visible view count at any cost;
- branding the entire future series as anime, lofi, relaxation, or sleep content.

## Audience Hypotheses

1. Ambient-cinema and slow-visual-art viewers will value the full audiovisual experience.
2. Experimental and environmental ambient-music listeners may watch longer than a broad relaxation audience.
3. Anime-background and Japanese-countryside viewers may be the strongest discovery audience for this particular film.
4. Reading, focus, art-making, and contemplation are more accurate use cases than sleep.
