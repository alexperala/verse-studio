# Growth Operating Cadence

## Active Launch

- Verify every live destination and supporting post.
- Capture the campaign's scheduled metric snapshots.
- Prioritize meaningful early comments and obvious publishing problems.
- Make no packaging change from anxiety alone; record the evidence and the expected effect.
- Keep one clearly named next action and owner in the campaign record.

## Weekly Growth Review

1. Review every active release and overdue follow-up.
2. Check content and channel performance since the previous review.
3. Separate genuine audience signals from vanity reach and very small samples.
4. Review the excerpt queue, curator or creator relationships, and open experiments.
5. Choose the one or two highest-value actions for the next seven days.
6. Record decisions and rejected ideas so they are not rediscovered without new evidence.

## Monthly Portfolio Review

- Compare releases by audience source, qualified watch time, returning behavior, and catalogue spillover.
- Identify which creative formats and audience promises are becoming recognizable.
- Review the balance of organic, earned, owned, and paid distribution.
- Close or revise experiments and update the positioning only when evidence is strong enough.
- Recommend the next distribution capability to build, not the next ten tactics to try.

## Recurring Automation

When Alex requests ongoing monitoring, schedule these reviews through Codex's recurring automation support. Automations may collect available metrics, update append-only records, flag comments, and draft a concise review. They may not publish, send outreach, change live accounts, or spend money unless the automation has explicit, bounded authority for that action.
