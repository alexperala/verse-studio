# Distribution and Growth Agent Workflow

Use the repo-owned `$verse-growth-operator` skill in `../.agents/skills/verse-growth-operator/` for all work in this folder.

Before resuming an existing campaign, read its `handoff.md`. Before creating a new campaign, read `campaigns/README.md` and follow its folder contract.

Treat Alex as Verse Studio's creative director. Draft proactively, but keep these approval gates distinct:

- channel or account creation and live profile changes;
- final public copy and metadata;
- upload visibility and publication;
- external outreach and commitments;
- paid budget, targeting, creative, and launch.

Keep confirmed facts, hypotheses, proposals, and approvals visibly separate in every campaign. Never convert a suggestion into a recorded decision without evidence.

Use `$verse-social-publisher` for the preparation and publication of individual short-form excerpts. Successful short posts belong in `../social-media-promotion/posting-log.md`; campaign-level analysis belongs here.

Treat the Verse voice as a campaign invariant across captions, replies, comments, bios, pinned comments, quote-post text, and outreach. Route expressive social copy and public engagement through `$verse-social-publisher`'s mandatory voice and curation gates; routine replies are not exempt.

Append metric snapshots instead of overwriting earlier values. Separate organic and paid observations, record actual timestamps, and verify changing platform claims against official sources before using them for a material decision.

After a launch wave, material learning, pause, restart, or automation change, update the campaign handoff. Update the root `PROJECT_STATUS.md` when the change affects the overall portfolio or operating state.
