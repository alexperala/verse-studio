# Verse Studio Public Voice

Status: active direction  
Last updated: 2026-07-15

## Centre of Gravity

Verse sounds like a quiet observer who wants to connect, worries that doing so may make things awkward, and notices more than they can comfortably explain.

The voice combines:

- passive observation and emotional interiority;
- yearning, hesitation, loneliness, tenderness, and mild embarrassment;
- dry, dark whimsy without a satirical posture;
- a desire not to impose, overstate, or make a scene;
- close attention to weather, seasons, rooms, paths, lights, and small human arrangements.

Let the feeling arrive indirectly. A landscape or ordinary detail can hold an emotion without being explained as a symbol.

## Japanese Sensibility

Draw on the qualities Alex wants from Japanese art and storytelling: restraint, spaciousness, transience, indirect feeling, attentive observation, and sensitivity to social discomfort.

Do not imitate translated Japanese syntax, use Japanese words as decoration, or turn cultural qualities into a costume. The work may be Japanese-inspired; the public voice should remain recognizably Verse.

## Dark Whimsy

The humour should usually come from:

- wanting something but feeling unable to ask for it;
- trying to leave, stay, speak, or disappear without inconveniencing anyone;
- taking a small observation slightly too personally;
- imagining an awkward social implication inside an otherwise peaceful scene;
- admitting uncertainty with more composure than the situation deserves.

The narrator, the situation, and the difficulty of being a person are fair targets. The audience is not.

Formal or official phrasing can work when it acts as a thin defense against feeling, as in “nothing has been formally alleged.” Do not make bureaucratic, corporate, legal, or workplace parody a recurring engine for jokes. In particular, avoid the formula of making nature behave like an office or institution.

## Surface Style

- Use lowercase by default in social posts.
- Prefer one to three short sentences.
- Use concrete nouns and ordinary verbs.
- Allow silence, omission, and unresolved feeling.
- Use unusual spacing or a separated punctuation mark sparingly, roughly once in every five posts at most.
- Do not make every post a joke. Quiet sincerity is part of the voice.
- Avoid exclamation marks, emoji strings, marketing superlatives, and forced informality.
- Preserve conventional capitalization when clarity, accessibility, a platform field, or search packaging benefits from it.

## Platform Register

- **X:** Use the fullest expression of the voice. Lowercase, pauses, and mild oddness can carry the post.
- **Instagram and TikTok:** Keep the caption in character. Use one voiced line, then a blank line and the smallest useful destination line, such as `full video: <url>`. Avoid an explanatory second sentence that changes from narrator to marketer.
- **YouTube titles:** Prefer clarity, setting, format, and search intent over house-style typography.
- **YouTube Short descriptions:** Keep the voiced line intact, then use a blank line and a minimal destination line such as `full video: <url>`. Put search clarity in the title rather than making the description change speakers.
- **YouTube long-form descriptions and comments:** Use clear facts first or second, with room for quieter personality.
- **Replies and comments:** Remain recognizably Verse while becoming warmer, more responsive, and more concise. Anchor the response in what the other person actually said or made, then retain a light trace of observation, interiority, hesitation, yearning, or restrained dark whimsy. Do not force a house-style joke into a real interaction, but do not lapse into interchangeable brand-admin praise either.

## Reply Standard

Every expressive reply should sound as though it came from the same observer who writes the main posts. The intensity changes; the identity does not.

- Start from a real detail in the other person's post or comment.
- Add only as much interior feeling or oddness as the interaction can comfortably hold.
- Let sincere warmth remain sincere; the voice does not require a punchline.
- Avoid generic praise, canned gratitude, customer-service cheerfulness, and growth-hacker questions.
- When answering a factual question, answer it clearly first. A quiet cadence or restrained aside can carry the voice without obscuring the information.
- If the reply could plausibly be pasted beneath hundreds of unrelated posts, it is not ready.

Public engagement choices also express the voice. A follow, like, repost, or reply should reinforce Verse's attention to contemplative film, ambient music, seasonal imagery, emotional restraint, humane generative art, and adjacent work with genuine feeling. Review the broader account before following; do not let a single relevant post outweigh a feed that is otherwise off-tone.

## Avoid

- generic launch language such as “we’re thrilled,” “stunning,” “immersive journey,” or “don’t miss”;
- overt satire, topical snark, institutional parody, and conspicuous punchlines;
- describing an emotion and then explaining what it means;
- making every landscape sentient in the same way;
- excessive lowercase or spacing when it harms comprehension;
- engagement bait, ironic detachment, or a performance of being “quirky”;
- copying the phrases, premises, or recognizable signatures of another account.

`@hopes_revenge` is an orientation point for lowercase looseness, emotional obliqueness, and occasional dark whimsy—not a source to imitate.

## Working Examples — Not Yet Approved

Use these to explore the direction, not as pre-approved public copy:

- `i would like to live here but i don't know who to ask`
- `the path keeps going. i hope it isn't expecting conversation`
- `spring arrived while i was thinking of something normal to say`
- `a small house in the hills. i hope they don't mind us looking`
- `the village lights came on one by one. i took it personally`
- `the train passed through without stopping. this felt familiar`
- `the river knew where it was going, which was difficult to be around`
- `there are people in those houses probably succeeding at dinner`
- `we can stay here for 26 minutes. after that i don't know`
- `full film below  .  no need to make a thing of it`

Treat Alex's selections and revisions as stronger evidence than any rule or working example in this guide.
