# Paid Discovery and Partnerships

## Order of Operations

1. Publish and establish an organic baseline.
2. Identify a specific uncertainty: audience, packaging, placement, or message.
3. Design the smallest test that could change a decision.
4. Obtain explicit approval for budget, creative, targeting, and launch.
5. Keep paid and organic results separate.
6. Stop, extend, or revise based on the predeclared success measure.

Paid distribution is an audience-learning tool, not a substitute for a catalogue or an organic strategy.

- In-feed video ads can appear in YouTube search, Watch Next, Home, and subscriptions. Adding content targeting prevents Home-feed delivery: <https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6227733>
- Google Ads supports targeting specific YouTube channels or videos and currently requires at least ten channel or video placements to save that selection: <https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7131506>

Reverify these claims before launching a campaign because ad products and eligibility rules change.

## First-Test Pattern

For a new Verse Ambient release, a reasonable proposal is a five-to-seven-day discovery test after roughly seven days of organic baseline, with a total budget in the CAD $75–150 range. This is a proposal, not standing spending authority.

Choose one primary hypothesis, such as:

- viewers of experimental ambient music respond better than general relaxation audiences;
- anime-background viewers click but environmental-music viewers watch longer;
- one title-thumbnail promise produces more qualified viewing than another.

Do not optimize solely for the cheapest view. Predeclare a useful outcome such as engaged-view cost, meaningful average view duration, relevant subscriber actions, or later organic return.

Placement targeting and in-feed inventory interact in ways that can narrow delivery. Confirm the current campaign subtype and available inventory in Google Ads before promising a particular placement-format combination.

## Curator and Creator Partnerships

Prefer a small number of genuine fits over bulk outreach. Suitable relationships may include:

- editorial recommendations or community posts;
- inclusion in a genuinely curated playlist;
- a shared excerpt with context;
- a guest mix, visual collaboration, or future co-created release;
- coverage by channels focused on ambient music, animation, visual art, or generative filmmaking.

Research the creator's actual work and audience before drafting. State the relevance in one or two specific sentences. Do not send generic praise, conceal compensation, promise exclusivity, or imply a relationship that does not exist.

Obtain Alex's approval before sending outreach, agreeing to terms, paying for placement, or making a public commitment. Record contact, date, proposal, status, compensation, and outcome in the campaign folder.

## Prohibited Tactics

- guaranteed views, subscribers, comments, or watch-time packages;
- undisclosed paid recommendations or playlist placements;
- spam comments or repetitive unsolicited messages;
- misleading metadata or irrelevant audience targeting;
- broad campaigns designed only to inflate the visible view count.
