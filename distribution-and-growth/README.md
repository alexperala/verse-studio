# Distribution and Growth

This is Verse Studio's audience-development workspace. Its purpose is to ensure that finished work receives thoughtful distribution, measurable follow-through, and a growing body of evidence about who values it.

Alex leads the art and creative direction. The growth operator owns the operational loop around the work:

1. understand the release and its intended experience;
2. position and package it for the right audience;
3. plan and execute an approved campaign;
4. coordinate excerpts through the social publisher;
5. track organic, earned, and paid performance separately;
6. turn results into a small number of useful next decisions.

## Structure

- `master-video-drop/` is the simple intake folder for a newly finished master video.
- `brand/` holds internal positioning and channel architecture.
- `campaigns/` gives each major release a brief, launch plan, package, approval state, qualitative findings, and current handoff. See [`campaigns/README.md`](campaigns/README.md) for the folder contract.
- `analytics/` holds append-only channel, content, and experiment records.
- `operating-cadence.md` defines the routine that keeps promotion alive after launch day.

## Campaign Handoffs

- [`campaigns/ambient-seasons-001/handoff.md`](campaigns/ambient-seasons-001/handoff.md) is the current read-first status for the first Verse Ambient release: completed work, evidence, failures, blockers, paused automations, and the recommended restart path.

Use the repo-owned `$verse-growth-operator` skill for work in this area. Public copy, account or channel creation, external outreach, publication, and paid spend each require the appropriate approval; one does not imply the others.

Passwords, session data, API keys, and recovery details do not belong in this repository.

## Simple Intake

Drop the finished master into `master-video-drop/`, then tell Codex that it is ready. No sidecar file or campaign brief is required. The growth operator will inspect the video, preserve the original, and recommend the smallest sensible release and promotion plan before creating derivatives or taking public action.
